Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 9, 2026. This website is operated by Evie Phillips LLC, based in Indiana, United States. Privacy and customer support: evie@eviephillips.com.

These policy drafts describe the current inquiry feature and proposed messaging terms. Automated messaging and 2FA are inactive. Legal review and operational messaging safeguards are still needed before those services go live.

1. Information We Collect

You may provide your name, email address, mobile phone number, and optional SMS consent through the contact form. A mobile phone number is optional unless you select SMS consent.

When you submit the contact form, your name, email address, optional mobile number, and SMS consent choice are transmitted to our server and stored in our database, together with the submission time and applicable consent disclosure. After a successful save, you are directed to a thank-you page. The form does not create an account or automatically send email or text messages. Contact evie@eviephillips.com with requests concerning your submitted information.

Account creation, interactive scheduling tools, and collection of event inputs are not currently active.

2. Mobile Information & SMS Privacy

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All other categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties under any circumstances.

3. Data Usage

Submitted information is used to respond to your inquiry and retain your communication preferences. Providing an email address or phone number alone does not authorize SMS messages; SMS consent is a separate, optional choice.

If the proposed messaging tools are activated, their purposes will include two-factor authentication (2FA) verification, requested calendar reminders, scheduling alerts, and family logistics notifications. Those messaging, account, and scheduling features are not currently active; this policy must be reviewed and updated to reflect their actual processing and retention before activation.
