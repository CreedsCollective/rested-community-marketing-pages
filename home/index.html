Meet Evie

Pulling up a chair for you.

think of me as your trusted confidant, slightly unhinged internet bestie, prayer partner + business/purpose mentor ♡

I’ve dated “the bad boy” and “the rich guy,” and almost married wrong three times. I’ve built big businesses, worked in 13 different industries, and been featured in Forbes twice, a blessing. But what I learned is that patterns matter more than potential, and impressive on paper doesn’t equal fulfilling in real life. Some of those lessons came with heartbreak. Others came with six figures in entrepreneurial tuition.

I eventually married the nerdy hot guy, and I’d choose him over and over again. But a ring, a headline, or another business win was never supposed to carry the weight of my whole identity. I let the timelines become my obsession, and I burnt out. There had to be more to living than waiting for the next thing to finally make me feel fulfilled.

I know what it’s like to trust God and still have questions about His scheduling.

He is already doing a lot in your life, even when the one thing you’re praying for still hasn’t happened. I’m here to help you see it and live it. You can share what’s breaking your heart and what you’re dreaming about building, in the same breath. We can cry, pray, laugh, and work through your next step. I’ll celebrate you, help you make a plan, and say the hard stuff with love. Being in your corner doesn’t mean agreeing with every decision, sis. It means caring enough to be honest.

Rest is holy to God. We don’t have to feel guilty for slowing down or confuse it with giving up. We can grow, build, and take action from a place of peace instead of panic.
