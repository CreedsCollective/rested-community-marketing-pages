2. SMS & Communications Program

The proposed SMS program covers automated account verification (2FA/OTP), calendar events, scheduling alerts, and operational reminders. If the program is activated, you may authorize those messages through a separate, optional SMS opt-in for the number you provide. Consent is not a condition of purchase.

Messaging is currently inactive. Submitting the form records your optional SMS consent choice and the applicable disclosure in our database; it does not trigger messages. An operational sender, applicable provider registration, consent safeguards, and working opt-out support must be in place before messaging begins.

  • Message Frequency: Message frequency varies based on user scheduling activity.
  • Rates: Standard message and data rates may apply.
  • Cancellation (Opt-Out): When the SMS program is active, you may opt out at any time by texting STOP to the sending number. You will receive one confirmation message upon unsubscribing, and no further program messages unless you opt in again.
  • Help & Support: When the SMS program is active, text HELP for assistance. You can currently contact our support team at evie@eviephillips.com.
